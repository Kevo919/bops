# Test: one-column web UI in a narrow window (phone 412x915, 420x800), before and after

- Kind: Live server (automated browser, in a Linux container, NOT on Kevin's PC or phone)
- By: Claude (subagent for the lead), 2026-11-05 08:07 to 09:21 UTC
- Code: before `windows-port` at f09bb55; after `feature/mobile-layout` at 69f9a50 (PR into windows-port, see below). Both `npx next build`, `next start` on 127.0.0.1.
- How it ran: the UI crawl's harness (tests/2026-11-05-0807-e2e-ui-crawl.md): self-hosted server with throwaway seeded data, a fake Orgo on 127.0.0.1, a random UI token, the paired phone's `bops_remote` cookie for 412x915 (touch, Android user agent), network blocked off loopback in both the browser and the server. Every screen of the crawl entered at 420x800 and at 412x915. One screen's path was added for a narrow window: New tab is reached through the chat's computer button (the panel is its own screen there).
- New check per screen: of the controls drawn in the window, how many can really be pressed (inside the window, not clipped by a column, not covered), and whether the page scrolls sideways.

## Result

**Before: 33 of 44 screens reached at 420 and at 412 (11 not reachable: chat More menu, routine editor and its Custom cadence, Skills, New tab, the bot panel, 5 bot settings pages). After: 44 of 44 at both sizes.** The 4 places not in this build (AI plans, Privacy, Your Apps, Domains) are not counted, as in the crawl.

Pass/fail: ✅ every screen reachable and full width on the phone; no page scrolls sideways at either size; the desktop window (1280x800) is unchanged.

- Controls pressable on the reached screens: before 1021/1385 at 412 (the chat column about 80 px wide, Settings' content past the right edge), after 638/703. What is left after: menus' own backdrops over the screen while a menu is open (chat More, a bot row's menu, the workspace menu), the message hover toolbars (React, Reply, Copy, Delete: hover-only, unchanged), and a few rows scrolled out of view inside a scrolling card (the routine's week strip, Memory's Ask first / Just do it). None is past the window's edge.
- Desktop: 1280x800 screenshots of 7 screens (a chat, a bot's profile in the panel, Routines, Settings Profile, Settings Bots, a bot's settings page, New chat or bot), before vs after, pixel diff: 4 identical; the other 3 differ only in message timestamps (the seed is relative to the time it ran) and the mascot's blink (the same screens differ by as much between two runs of the old build: 1622 and 1936 px).
- New test `scripts/test-mobile-layout.mjs` (Playwright on the production build, made-up Orgo, no network): 28/28 pass on the branch; on the old build 13 fail (every phone check) and the desktop check passes.

## What the phone shows now

- The list of chats is the whole window (sidebar without its collapse button). Opening a chat shows it full width with a round Back button top left; the chat's computer button opens the side panel as its own screen, with a bar: Back and the tabs (scroll sideways), New tab.
- Routines, Knowledge, Issues, a bot's profile open in the panel screen; opened from the list, its Back goes to the list.
- Settings opens at its nav, full width; each place is full width with "< Settings" on top; Back on the nav leaves Settings. A bot's settings page is one column, its sections a row of chips.
- Touch targets 40 px or more for the bars, Back buttons, Settings nav rows, sidebar rows, header buttons and footer, tabs and their close buttons. Small in-card chips and links (routine filters, Done/Drop on an idea) are still under 40 px.
- `viewport-fit=cover`, the app padded with `env(safe-area-inset-*)`.

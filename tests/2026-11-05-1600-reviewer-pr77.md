# PR #77 review: easy phone pairing (independent reviewer, 2026-11-05 16:00 UTC, head 9cee470)

**Verdict:** REQUEST CHANGES. Small fixes; the builder is on them.

## What passed
- The code is shown once.
- Pair again and Unpair ask first and lock out the old phone.
- No home-wifi address is offered.
- The QR images are made by the server only.
- The "Your phone" item and /api/remote are limited to the owner's window. Shared workspaces can't see them.
- A scanned link goes through the same confirm step.
- The scanner needs no camera permission.
- Nav wiring is fine.

## Fixes asked
1. The phone app accepts extra parts in a pairing link. It isn't exploitable, because of the confirm step, but it breaks the rule.
2. "Pair a phone" works even with no Tailscale address, which leaves a "Paired" card that no phone can reach.
3. At phone width (412px), the pairing QR is cut off.
4. Nit: step 2 can show a different address than the QR.
5. Nit: the QR images are rebuilt every time Settings opens.
